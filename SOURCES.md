## 8. Gaps flagged in the paper

1. **“Blue-eyed barbarian” (碧眼胡) first occurrence not pinned.** Graded in BOD as standard by the Song lamp literature, “attested by 1004, earlier occurrence unverified.” Paper §3 (table) and §10 carry exactly that grade and no earlier date. Source: BOD header; §1.6.
2. **The *Baolin Zhuan* is lost.** Known through quotation, so 801 attributions are one notch softer than an extant text. Paper §§2.5, 7.1 and 10 carry the qualification. Source: BOD header; §1.5.
3. **Secondary-only details flagged in place,** per BOD's evidence-base note: the “over 180” age in secondary summaries of Daoxuan (BOD §1.3; paper §2.3) and the unverified practitioner claim about Dōgen's *Shōbōgenzō* ch. 50 (BOD §4.4; paper §7.4).
